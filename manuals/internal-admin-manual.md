# AfriCred Internal Admin Manual

## 1. Start With Alerts

Open the AfriCred Admin Centre and review unread notifications first. Alerts highlight pending reviews, expired documents, billing risk, scan spikes, and account status changes.

## 2. Manage Requirements

Use Requirements to create, edit, disable, or archive dynamic compliance rules. Each requirement can affect profile completion, verification badge eligibility, submission blocking, and good-standing effects.

## 3. Review Accounts

Open Reviews, inspect the account profile, submitted documents, completion score, billing status, and verification request. Approve accounts only when the required evidence is current and trustworthy.

## 4. Give Clear Feedback

When rejecting documents or requesting account changes, use the custom feedback dialog. Keep notes specific, calm, and actionable so institutions know exactly what to fix.

## 5. Billing And Good Standing

Use Billing to record manual payments and review payment status. If billing or compliance rules affect issuing or public verification, communicate the next step before applying strict account actions.

## 6. Audit And Analytics

Use Analytics to monitor platform health, plan usage, verification scans, and compliance trends. Use Audit logs for sensitive actions, requirement changes, account decisions, and admin activity.

## 7. Support Handover

For onboarding, confirm that the institution can log in, choose a structure, add records, use Builder, issue with QRIssuer, and verify public records. Point teams to the public user manual for day-to-day workflows.
